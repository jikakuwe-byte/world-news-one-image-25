The Strait of Hormuz, 25 November 2026: two tankers ablaze after striking mines, in the day’s gravest threat to world energy shipping. Illustration.

RIYADH TOWER HIT, REFINERY ABLAZE

Yemen’s Houthis said they attacked Saudi Arabia’s Ras Tanura refinery, a day after a strike on a Riyadh skyscraper injured 11 people. The Red Sea’s other shipping lane is under fire too.

GAZA: HAMAS SECURITY CHIEF KILLED

Israel’s military killed Hamas colonel Mohammad Abuelouf, who headed the militants’ interior security service, along with his wife and two children, Gaza medics said.

HUAWEI CONVICTED IN U.S. TRIAL

A federal court found the Chinese tech giant guilty of conspiracy to steal trade secrets and bank fraud — but not of the overarching racketeering charge.

PAKISTAN REACHES ORBIT

China’s Shenzhou-24 docked at its space station carrying Pakistan’s first astronaut — a historic first for the country in the new race to the Moon.

POPE LAMENTS COP31 FAILURE

Pope Leo XIV voiced concern as the U.N. climate talks ended without agreement, warning extreme weather linked to the climate crisis grows more frequent.

SUDAN: 19 KILLED IN DRONE STRIKES

Rapid Support Forces drone strikes killed 19 civilians and wounded 20 in two cities, Sudanese human rights group Emergency Lawyers said.

CAIRO TANKER FIRE KILLS 31

A fuel tanker crash ignited a massive fire on a highway outside Cairo, killing at least 31 people, Egypt’s Health Ministry said.
